@VMFA 225 Vikings @421st FS Black Widows

**Hitman now passes the 9-line in three blocks, and you read each one back** 🎯

Check in with Hitman 1-1 (61.000 FM) or 2-1 (62.000 FM) as before. He gives you the target, then the 9-line three lines at a time:
```
Lines 1-3   IP, heading, distance          -> you read back
Lines 4-6   elevation, target, location    -> you read back
Lines 7-9   mark, friendlies, egress       -> you read back
Hitman: "Confirmed. Report IP inbound."
```
• Read back wrong and he says *"negative, readback incorrect"* and gives you the same block again
• Missed it? *"Say again"* repeats the block. *"Say again line 6"* repeats one line
• Lines 4-6 carry the grid, read digit by digit: *"three seven Tango, Golf Juliet, three one eight two seven, seven zero four five seven"*
• Lines 7-9 just need a *"copy"* (or the friendlies / egress back)
• Don't say "Hitman" halfway through your readback; lead with it or leave it out
• Everything after "confirmed" (IP inbound, in, cleared hot, off, BDA) is unchanged

Same lines as the kneeboard card, so read along. Works for every target, Black Sea and the TIC mission.
