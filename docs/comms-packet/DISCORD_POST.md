**SkyEye ATC: the tower now hands you off at 7 miles** 🛫

**What changed on departure**
• After takeoff the tower says: *"proceed to angels five, expect handoff at seven DME."*
• At 7 miles the tower calls **you**:
> "Raider 302, Senaki Departure, seven miles, angels five, leaving my airspace. Contact command, two eight two point zero, channel one. Safe skies, g'day."
• Then switch to Command (282.0) and check in. That's it.

**Do I still call 7 DME?**
No need. If you do anyway, the tower answers *"copy"*. If you call before 7 miles, you get the handoff right then.

**Also**
• "Clear the active" right after takeoff now counts as your airborne call.
• The tower reads your real altitude from Tacview, so "angels" is where you actually are.
• Australian-accent towers sign off "g'day"; British ones "good day".
