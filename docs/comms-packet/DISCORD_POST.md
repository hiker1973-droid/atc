**Hitman update** 🎯

From Raider 302's session on Hitman 2-1:
• **Request BDA** works any time. Before an attack: *"negative BDA, no attack yet"*. Bombs still flying: *"standby for BDA"*
• **Hit the wrong target?** He now tells you where your bombs landed: *"impacted on target 9, not target 5"*, instead of "no impacts observed"
• **Request direct attack** after a pass sends you at the **same target** again, unless it is destroyed. Call *"target destroyed"* and he copies it and gives you a new one next time
• **Done?** Say *"winchester"* or *"off station"* and he releases you from the net (BDA first if one is due)
• He now hears *"Demand 21"* and *"Hit man"* as Hitman, and *"reporting to IP Knife"* counts as IP inbound
• Line 6 (the grid) is read a little slower again
