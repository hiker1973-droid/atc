# Readback ATC 0.2.0

- Reports: after-action reports from the server's dcs.log and Tacview - maps, engagements, every shot, kills and losses, safety items - as a branded PDF, plus a Discord post you can copy.
- Stats tab: greenie board, flight stats per pilot, CSV and PDF export. ElevenLabs credits moved here.
- Dashboard: colour modes (Dark, Light, Night, Auto), a tidier two-row header, panels that hide when empty.
- Licence: the dashboard shows your licence or trial days; a 14-day renewal reminder and 7 days' grace after a key ends.
- Installer: a proper setup window, and you choose the install folder.
- Tankers answer "you are cleared for 4,000 pounds" and "cleared contact"; Marshal copes better with garbled calls; Paddles has a new voice.
- Fixes: the air picture is blank until you set your own SRS and Tacview addresses; the Roles table's Save buttons stay in view.
