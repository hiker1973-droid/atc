# Readback ATC 0.2.5

- Settings > Roles > Other stations: the Add list can now make, beside a new tanker:
  - a JTAC, with its own callsign, frequency, range sector (or the whole range) and voice. Command sends tasked flights to it like Hitman 1-1 and 2-1.
  - a second AWACS, with its own callsign, frequency and voices, in its own window.
  - a second Command, with its own name, frequency and voices, in its own window. The tankers, JTACs and handoffs stay with the theatre's own Command.
- A second AWACS or Command starts and stops with its theatre and shows on the dashboard.
- A name or frequency already used in the theatre is refused.
