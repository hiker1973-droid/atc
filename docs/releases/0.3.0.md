# Readback ATC 0.3.0 - Public beta

Readback ATC is open to squadrons outside vSFG-7. It puts towers, ATIS, Command, an AWACS, Deckboss, Marshal, Paddles, tankers and JTACs on your SRS frequencies; they hear the call and answer by voice from the mission's weather and the live air picture.

- Licences are free during the beta: every install starts with a 14-day trial, and a squadron that asks on our Discord gets a 90-day key.
- Needs: a Windows PC running your SRS server (External AWACS Mode on) and an OpenAI key. Tacview real-time is recommended; ElevenLabs and Claude keys are optional.
- Flown weekly on Caucasus and Syria. Persian Gulf, Germany, Iraq and Kola are included and less flown.

New since 0.2.6:
- Marshal answers "request push time" in Case III with your approach time, or a new one if yours has passed.
- Case III approach times are always a whole minute, at least a minute apart from every other jet in the stack.
- The User Manual, comms packet and phraseology reference carry the new Case III calls.

Problems: Help > Report a problem, then post the zip in #bug-reports on our Discord.
