# Readback ATC 0.3.2

**Case III (Marshal)**
- Commencing gets "radar contact" with the platform and final bearing, the way a carrier's approach control answers it.
- Marshal calls "eight miles, dirty up" on final from the radar picture.
- Bolter and wave-off pattern: go round in Case III and Marshal tells you "climb to one thousand two hundred, at four miles turn downwind, report abeam". Say "abeam" and you are turned back to final, and handed to Paddles again at three miles. Say "bolter" or "wave off" yourself and you get the same.
- Approach times in the stack are now ten minutes apart.

**ATIS**
- Each ATIS read is recorded once and replayed whenever the same weather and runway come round again, instead of being recorded fresh every time. Far fewer voice calls, so a much smaller OpenAI bill. A replayed read keeps the information letter it was recorded with.
- In light wind the runway now changes once a day, at 04:00 Mountain time, instead of every four hours.
