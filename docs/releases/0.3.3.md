# Readback ATC 0.3.3

**Towers**
- A call that starts with your callsign is answered: "Venom 212, tower, Venom 212 airborne".
- "Flight of two F-16s inbound" with no callsign gets "say again with your full callsign", instead of the tower using "flight of two F-16s" as your name.
- "Venom 0-2-0, 2-ship" is read as Venom 020, a flight of two, not Venom 0202.
- Senaki also answers to the speech-recognition slips "Scooby Tower" and "Sukhoi Tower".

**Command**
- Calls meant for a tower, Ground or traffic that land on Command's frequency are no longer answered by Command.
- Ask Command for a picture or bogey dope and it tells you the AWACS frequency.
- "Commander" works as Command's name.
