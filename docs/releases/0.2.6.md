# Readback ATC 0.2.6

- Carrier Case III recovery, reworked against the squadron's Case III kneeboard:
  - Marshal gives a proper holding fix: marshal on the final bearing's reciprocal, at a DME of angels + 15, from angels 6 up ("marshal on the 263, 21 DME, angels 6, expected approach time is 23").
  - A jet that reports established after its approach time gets a new one.
  - At three miles Marshal hands the jet to Paddles: "contact paddles, call the ball".
  - Paddles answers a three-mile initial or check-in with "call the ball at three-quarters of a mile".
  - Needles, coupled and command control reports are answered; negative needles gets "fly bullseye". After commencing these calls may start with your modex.
  - "Commencing, altimeter 30.16, state 9.5" is read as commencing, not as a fuel report.
- Towers no longer switch runways under a jet: with light wind, the runway rotation waits while anyone is taxiing, holding short, taking off or inbound.
