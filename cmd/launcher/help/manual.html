User Manual

How to run Readback ATC on your DCS World server, set it up the way your squadron flies, and talk to its controllers.

1. The controllers

Each controller is a station on SRS that listens on its frequency and answers your pilots by voice.

StationWhat it does
TowersOne per airfield: radio checks, startup, taxi, takeoff, departure handoff, the overhead break and landing, go-arounds, emergencies, traffic alerts.
ATISA recorded weather and runway broadcast for each field, kept in step with its tower.
CommandThe strike net: check-in, fence in/out, tasking, tankers, recoveries and diverts.
AWACS (GCI)Picture, bogey dope, declare, snaplock, threat calls and vectors, from the air picture Tacview sees.
Marshal, Deckboss, PaddlesCarrier operations: the marshal stack and approach, the deck and catapults, and the LSO on the ball.
Tankers, JTACAir-to-air refuelling check-in and join-up, and close air support 9-lines, where your start files include them.

2. The dashboard

Open Readback ATC from the Start menu, or http://localhost:7000.

  • Theatre buttons pick the map. Start runs every controller in it; Stop stops them all.
  • Cards show each controller, its frequency, and whether it is running, with Start/Stop for each.
  • Tower status shows each field's runway, weather and traffic.
  • Logs shows what the controllers heard and said, live.
  • Banners at the top show the trial days left, an available update, or that Setup is not finished.

Changes reach a controller when it restarts: use Restart this theatre's roles under Roles, or Stop and Start on the dashboard. Tell your pilots when you move a frequency.

Each tower card on the dashboard has a Blue/Red switch: a red field's tower talks to the red coalition on SRS and hands its departures to Red Command, for servers that run one.

4. Talking to Readback

  • Tune the station's frequency (AM on UHF and VHF, FM below 88 MHz).
  • Say the station first, then your callsign, then the request: "Batumi Tower, Viper 1-1, request taxi."
  • Use a two-part callsign such as Viper 1-1 or a modex such as Raider 302, and keep it the same all flight.
  • Release the transmit key and wait for the answer. If it didn't hear you, ask "say again" or call again.

The calls below are the ones Readback is tested against. Similar wordings work too.

5. Frequencies

Towers use each field's DCS tower frequency; Settings > Frequencies lists them for your theatre. The standard start files put Command on 282.000, the AWACS on 264.000 (or what you chose in Setup), Marshal on 306.300 and Deckboss on 128.600. Give your pilots these on their kneeboard or preset card, and update them whenever you change one in Settings.

6. When something is wrong

  • No answer: the controller must be running (dashboard), the pilot on its frequency, and the call must start with the station's name. The Logs page shows what it heard.
  • It heard something else: speak after the transmit key is down, a little slower, station first. Check SRS isn't clipping your microphone.
  • No voice at all: send the test call in Setup (step 5); it reports exactly what is missing.
  • Command or AWACS can't see aircraft: check Tacview's real-time telemetry is on and its address is right in Settings.
