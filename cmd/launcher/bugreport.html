Report a problem

Tell us what went wrong. Readback puts your description together with its logs and settings in one zip file, which you post in #bug-reports on the vSFG-7 Discord. Nothing is sent from here.

  • In the zip: your description, the Readback and Windows versions, the licence state, the roles and their status, the settings, your changes to roles, frequencies and sides, and the last part of every role's log.
  • Your OpenAI, ElevenLabs and Claude keys, the SRS password and the licence key are never included: they are removed from everything, logs too.
  • The logs do include pilot callsigns and what was said on the radio.

Now post it

  1. The zip has downloaded: (in your Downloads folder).
  2. Copy this message:
  3. In #bug-reports on the vSFG-7 Discord, paste the message and attach the zip.
