Flight stats

Every player who flew in the period, from the DCS server's own logs. Click a heading to sort. Hours run from takeoff to landing, crash or ejection; a jet left in the air (re-slotting) counts until the pilot's next takeoff.

Greenie board

Every carrier pass the DCS LSO graded, oldest first; the number is the wire. Ranked by average grade points (_OK_ 5, OK 4, (OK) 3, B 2.5, --- 2, WO 1, C 0; a foul-deck wave-off is not scored).

Where the numbers come from

The DCS server's dcs.log files on this PC (the live log, dcs.log.old and the dated .zip copies DCS keeps), and any server logs uploaded here. A game client's dcs.log has no events to count.
